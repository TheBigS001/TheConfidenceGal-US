I Know Exactly What It Feels Like

To work incredibly hard, consistently deliver results, and still watch leadership opportunities go to someone else.

When I moved to the UK, I believed hard work alone would get me there. It didn't. For years, I stayed stuck while others, sometimes with less experience, moved ahead. I was capable but I simply didn't understand how leadership readiness was really judged inside large organisations.

Once I did, everything changed.

Over the next four years, I earned three consecutive promotions, eventually becoming a Managing Director at NatWest Group. More importantly, I stopped being seen as the person who delivered the work and started being trusted to lead it.

Today, I help experienced women in banking, finance and technology make that same transition. Professionals from 45 organisations across the UK have worked with me to secure promotions, communicate with greater influence, build executive presence and confidently step into bigger leadership roles.

I am not here to tell you to "speak up more" or simply become more confident. I will show you why capable women professionals become stuck and how leadership decisions are actually formed inside organisations like yours.
